Before the Workshop

How to Prepare

What to Gather
  • 2–3 litres warm drinking water
  • Rock salt or Himalayan salt
  • Large container or jug for mixing
  • Yoga mat & comfortable loose clothing
  • Rice, yellow moong dal, ghee (for post-practice khichdi)
  • Towel and tissues nearby
Diet — 2 Days Before
  • Eat khichdi, steamed vegetables, soups, soft cooked rice
  • Avoid fried, spicy, processed, or heavy non-vegetarian food
  • No alcohol, coffee, or excessive tea
  • Dinner the night before — light meal, taken before 8 PM
Day of Workshop
  • Attend on a completely empty stomach (10–12 hr fast)
  • No food, tea, or coffee before the session
  • Small sips of plain water are acceptable
  • Join from a quiet space with easy washroom access
Setup & Environment
  • Keep camera on throughout (required for safety supervision)
  • Ensure washroom is immediately accessible
  • Let someone at home know you are doing this practice
  • Keep oral rehydration solution available as a precaution
Health & Safety

Is This Right
For You?

Varisara is a powerful therapeutic practice and is not suitable for everyone. Please read this carefully before registering. A declaration form will be shared with all participants prior to the session.

Participants with any medical conditions should consult their doctor before attending. This workshop is a wellness practice and is not a substitute for medical treatment.
Severe hypertension or heart disease
Kidney disease or kidney-related conditions
Pregnancy (any trimester)
Recent abdominal surgery or major injuries
Active stomach or intestinal ulcers, hernia
Severe back, spine, or joint conditions
Severe fever, diarrhea, or acute weakness
Chronic inflammatory bowel disease (IBD/IBS-severe)

No prior yoga experience is needed. The five asanas used in Varisara are simple standing and floor movements that most people can perform comfortably. Your instructor will demonstrate each movement at the start of the session and guide you through every step in real time.

Stop the practice immediately and inform the instructor. If you experience severe dizziness, vomiting, sharp abdominal pain, or extreme weakness — stop without hesitation. The instructor is trained to handle these situations, and safety protocols are in place.
