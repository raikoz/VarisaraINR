The Practice

What is
Shankhprakshalan?

Shankhprakshalan — derived from "Shankha" (conch shell) and "Prakshalana" (to wash thoroughly) — is one of the most powerful yogic cleansing practices described in the ancient Hatha Yoga Pradipika.

The practice involves drinking warm saline water in structured rounds, interspersed with five specific yoga asanas that move the water progressively through the entire digestive tract — from stomach to colon — flushing it clean.

Unlike modern detox trends, Shankhprakshalan is a physiologically grounded, systematically structured practice. When done correctly under guidance, it resets gut microbiome ecology, reduces bloating, supports hormonal balance, and leaves participants feeling remarkably light.

This 2-hour live workshop brings this traditionally in-person practice to your home, with real-time instructor supervision and a full pre- and post-protocol support system.

Before the Workshop

How to Prepare

🛒
What to Gather
  • 2–3 litres warm drinking water
  • Rock salt or Himalayan salt
  • Large container or jug for mixing
  • Yoga mat & comfortable loose clothing
  • Rice, yellow moong dal, ghee (for post-practice khichdi)
  • Towel and tissues nearby
🍽️
Diet — 2 Days Before
  • Eat khichdi, steamed vegetables, soups, soft cooked rice
  • Avoid fried, spicy, processed, or heavy non-vegetarian food
  • No alcohol, coffee, or excessive tea
  • Dinner the night before — light meal, taken before 8 PM
⏰
Day of Workshop
  • Attend on a completely empty stomach (10–12 hr fast)
  • No food, tea, or coffee before the session
  • Small sips of plain water are acceptable
  • Join from a quiet space with easy washroom access
📹
Setup & Environment
  • Keep camera on throughout (required for safety supervision)
  • Ensure washroom is immediately accessible
  • Let someone at home know you are doing this practice
  • Keep oral rehydration solution available as a precaution
Health & Safety

Is This Right
For You?

Shankhprakshalan is a powerful therapeutic practice and is not suitable for everyone. Please read this carefully before registering. A declaration form will be shared with all participants prior to the session.

🫀 Severe hypertension or heart disease
🫘 Kidney disease or kidney-related conditions
🤰 Pregnancy (any trimester)
🔪 Recent abdominal surgery or major injuries
🩺 Active stomach or intestinal ulcers, hernia
🦴 Severe back, spine, or joint conditions
🌡️ Severe fever, diarrhea, or acute weakness
🧬 Chronic inflammatory bowel disease (IBD/IBS-severe)
Participants with any medical conditions should consult their doctor before attending. This workshop is a wellness practice and is not a substitute for medical treatment.
